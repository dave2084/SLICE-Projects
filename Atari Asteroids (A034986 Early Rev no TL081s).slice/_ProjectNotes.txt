This early revision Asteroids board layout differes from the more common variants in two ways:

1.  The X and Y analog output stages do not have a TL081.
2.  Row 10 ICs A10 -> F10 are 74LS399, the later layout replaces this with extra ICs squeezed bewteen the rows.

The board sampled here is marked A034986 on the front and Rev B on the rear, use the first printing of DP-143 for schematics.

The easiest way to identify this is the lack of TL081s.
